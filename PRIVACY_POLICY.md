# Privacy Policy - Window Seat

*Last updated: September 28, 2026*

Window Seat is a flight companion app that tells you what you are flying over.

## Location
- The app uses your device location, while the app is open, to work out what you are flying over.
- Location is processed **on your phone only**. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is not kept after the flight.
- If you deny the permission, the app follows a built-in flight clock instead of GPS.

## Flight number lookup (optional)
- If you type a flight number and tap Find, that flight number is sent to the free public route database adsbdb.com to look up the airports.
- Nothing else is sent (no location, no device identifiers).
- Choosing your airports yourself works fully offline.

## Stored on your device
- Settings (theme, voice, speaking speed) and a short list of recent routes. Clear it in Settings or by uninstalling.

## What we do not do
- No account, no name/email collection, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no sale or sharing of data.

## Voice
- Narrations are read with your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. Nothing is sent anywhere.
